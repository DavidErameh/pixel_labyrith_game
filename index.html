HOW TO PLAY

⚔️ Space = Sword strike (dash-lunge)
🛡️ Hold Shift/Q = Shield (3 hits before cooldown)
❤️ Collect hearts to restore HP
🗝️ Find keys to unlock the exit door
🗺️ Press M or tap Mini-map to expand full map
🔮 Touch Runestone Shrines to save & heal
👑 Beware Mini-Bosses near Shrines & Malakor
💀 Enemies counter-attack & recover fast!
🔥 Space = Fireball (10 MP) — ranged, 4 tiles
💥 E / 🔥 btn = Fire Burst AOE (25 MP, 2 tile radius)
✨ Hold Shift/Q = Arcane Barrier (20 MP, 2 hits)
⚡ MP regens +2/sec passively, +8 per enemy killed
🗝️ Find keys to unlock the exit door

🔮 Shrines restore +2 HP +15 MP & save progress
⚠️ Wizard has only 10 HP — manage MP carefully!
