PIXEL
LABYRINTH

⚔️ The Sunken Vaults of Oakhaven ⚔️

Descend through five cursed floors. Uncover the truth of the Hollow Warden.

Use WASD / Arrow Keys on PC  |  D-Pad on Mobile

HOW TO PLAY

🗡️ Attack enemies by moving into them
❤️ Collect hearts to restore HP
💎 Grab gems & coins for score
🗝️ Find keys to unlock the exit door
⚡ Power-ups give temporary boosts
🗺️ Mini-map (top-right) shows explored areas
👑 Defeat the boss to advance floors
💀 Enemies respawn each new floor

CONTROLS

⌨️ PC: WASD / Arrow Keys to move
Space to attack / interact
📱 Mobile: On-screen D-Pad joystick
