HOW TO PLAY

Space = Sword strike (dash-lunge)
Shift/Q = Toggle Shield (5 hits before cooldown)
Collect hearts to restore HP
Find keys to unlock the exit door
Press M or tap Mini-map to expand full map
Touch Runestone Shrines to save & heal
Beware Red Knights & the Dark Knight on Floor 6
Enemies counter-attack & recover fast!
Space = Fireball (10 MP) — ranged, 4 tiles
E / Burst btn = Fire Burst AOE (25 MP, 2 tile radius)
Shift/Q = Toggle Arcane Barrier (20 MP, 2 hits)
MP regens +2/sec passively, +8 per enemy killed
Find keys to unlock the exit door
Press M to expand dungeon map
Shrines restore +2 HP +15 MP & save progress
Wizard has only 10 HP — manage MP carefully!
